# Luna & Co. — customer support guide

This guide answers the most common questions about orders, shipping, returns, sizing, and caring for your jewelry.

## Returns and refunds
We accept returns within 14 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, in their original packaging, with the authenticity card included. Once we receive and inspect the returned item, your refund is issued to the original payment method within 5 business days. Custom or engraved pieces are final sale and cannot be returned.

## Shipping
We offer free standard shipping on all orders over $75 within the United States. Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping (1 to 2 business days) is available at checkout for a flat $15. International shipping is available to most countries and typically takes 7 to 14 business days; import duties are the customer's responsibility.

## Ring sizing
If you are unsure of your ring size, we can mail you a free ring sizer before you order. If your ring doesn't fit, we offer one free resizing within 60 days of purchase for most styles. Eternity bands and tension settings cannot be resized due to their construction.

## Materials and care
Our fine jewelry is made from solid 14k and 18k gold, platinum, and sterling silver, with ethically sourced diamonds and gemstones. Store your pieces separately to avoid scratches, remove them before swimming or showering, and clean them gently with a soft cloth. Avoid contact with perfume, lotion, and household chemicals.

## Order tracking
Once your order ships, you will receive a confirmation email with a tracking number. You can also view your order status anytime by logging into your account and visiting the Orders page. If your tracking hasn't updated in more than 3 business days, please contact us.

## Warranty
All our jewelry comes with a lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects, covering issues like loose stones or clasp failure under normal wear. The warranty does not cover accidental damage, misuse, or normal wear such as scratches. Proof of purchase is required for all warranty claims.

## Payment options
We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and Apple Pay. We also offer interest-free installments on orders over $100, split into four payments. All transactions are secured with SSL encryption.

## Gift options
Every order arrives in a signature gift box at no extra charge. You can add a personalized gift message at checkout, and we offer gift receipts that do not show pricing. Gift cards are available from $50 to $500 and never expire.

## Contact us
Our support team is available Monday through Friday, 9am to 6pm EST. You can reach us by email at support@lunaco.example or through the live chat on our website. We typically respond to emails within one business day.
